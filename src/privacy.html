Privacy at GeneoGraph

This notice covers the GeneoGraph website, contact form, Research Group signup and interactive demo.

Updated 26 November 2026

Who handles your information

The GeneoGraph project team manages the website and its contact records. For privacy requests, contact geneograph@gmail.com.

Research Group signup

Joining is optional. By submitting your email, you consent to Research Group updates and invitations to testing (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Web3Forms forwards your signup to our inbox. We keep the contact address for this purpose until you withdraw consent or the group ends. You can withdraw by emailing us at any time; this does not affect processing before withdrawal. You can try the demo without joining.

Contact messages

The contact form collects your email, subject and message. We use them to respond and follow up, based on our legitimate interest in handling inquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We retain correspondence as needed for that purpose, subject to applicable legal obligations. Providing these fields is necessary to send the form, but you are not required to contact us. Do not send sensitive personal or family research records through this form.

Hosting and form services

GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Hosting requests also involve ordinary technical metadata. The landing pages use locally hosted Manrope fonts and do not load advertising or analytics scripts. The landing code does not set tracking cookies or use browser storage.

Web3Forms, operated by Web3Creative, receives form submissions and technical metadata and delivers messages to the GeneoGraph email inbox. Its published data processing agreement describes processing on behalf of the form operator, infrastructure providers and anti-spam processing. Its service may send submitter IP and email information to spam-prevention providers. The provider states that submission records may be retained for up to three years unless removed sooner; this is separate from our mailbox retention.

International processing

Hosting, email, form and demo providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Web3Forms operates from India and its published data processing agreement includes EU standard contractual clauses for applicable international transfers. See its agreement and the provider notices for details; contact us for information about applicable safeguards or a copy of them.

Interactive demo

The interactive demo processes project records and imported files in your browser, without a GeneoGraph project storage backend. Research changes are session-based and reset on reload; files you explicitly download remain on your device. Browser storage can retain language, theme, last-project information, tree display settings and tour status. You can clear these using your browser settings.

The online demo loads Quill and MapLibre assets from jsDelivr and unpkg. Map styles, tiles, icons and fonts are loaded from OpenFreeMap; tile requests reflect the displayed map area. External requests may reveal IP addresses and ordinary browser/request metadata. Place autocomplete sends entered place text and interface language to Geoapify and, when needed, LocationIQ. Manual place entry does not require autocomplete. If you choose to download supported linked GEDCOM photos, requests go to the MyHeritage photo host (sites-cf.mhcache.com), not an upload of the entire GEDCOM file. Avoid including confidential information in external searches or using links you are not permitted to access.

Your rights and choices

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, and withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, unsubscribe from Research Group messages or ask about our handling of data, email geneograph@gmail.com. You may also complain to your competent data protection authority, including the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
